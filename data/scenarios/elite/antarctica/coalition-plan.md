## Strategic Rationale

**Priority resources: Supplies and Electronics**

- Australia + New Zealand give two pop 6 supply capitals under active management
- Indonesia's pop 6 electronics capital is the standout pick — rare to have this at tier 6
- Norway's fuel capital supports heavy air force upkeep costs
- Ukraine captured (not played) because its pop 6 rares income complements the active players' supply/electronics focus — and UK's pop 6 supply would be redundant alongside Australia and NZ

**No navy** — coalition is air-force focused; naval build is not planned for this scenario
